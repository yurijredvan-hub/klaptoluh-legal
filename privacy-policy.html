Klaptoluh Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

This policy covers Klaptoluh, the offline farming puzzle game with Android package com.klaptoluh.game, published by [REQUIRED: DEVELOPER/PUBLISHER NAME].

Information handled by the game

Klaptoluh does not send personal information to us or operate developer servers. The reviewed release has no in-app accounts, advertisements, analytics SDK, chat, online multiplayer or purchases for real money. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

The game stores your farm layout, production progress, inventory, coins, medals, upgrades, language and sound/motion settings locally on your device. A local backup file helps recover progress if the main file is damaged.

Collection and sharing

The game does not transmit this local progress or personal data to the developer or third parties. We do not sell user information. Google Play and Android may independently process information or provide system backups under their own policies and your device settings; those platform services are not developer-operated game servers.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you clear the game's storage or uninstall it, subject to your device's backup and restore settings. Clearing storage removes your progress and preferences. There is no game account or server-side profile to delete.

Children and future changes

The game does not collect personal information from players of any age. The publisher must select the intended age groups and comply with applicable Google Play requirements. If a future release adds data-handling features, this policy and the Play Data safety declaration will be reviewed and updated.
